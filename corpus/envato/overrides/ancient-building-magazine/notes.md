# ancient-building-magazine

12-page A4 magazine layout. Declared fonts (all OFL on Google
Fonts): Poppins, DM Sans, Instrument Serif. We substitute with
Inter + SourceSerif4 from `corpus/fonts/`. Display weights (40+pt
headlines) show notable letter-shape drift since Inter is a more
geometric face than Poppins.

Stage: `smoke` — large geometric headlines + tabbed body text are
both layout paths we want broad coverage of but not yet gated.
