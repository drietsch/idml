# newspaper

28 MB tabloid layout — heaviest IDML in the envato corpus. Likely
to surface text-layout edge cases (large multi-column flow, kerning
under headlines, drop-caps). Useful as a smoke fixture even if it
never makes it to `gated`.

The Read-Me.txt is a marketing pitch with no font list; the
mappings here are derived from inspecting the IDML's stories.
