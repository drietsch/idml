# catalog

Product catalog template. Declared fonts: Playfair Display + Montserrat
(both OFL via Google Fonts).

We don't ship a high-contrast display serif comparable to Playfair;
Cormorant Garamond is the closest match in the bundle (also a
bookish serif but with lower contrast). Heading-heavy pages will
show shape drift more than body-text pages.

Stage: `smoke`.
